# Bot commands

Commands are case-insensitive. In groups, the bot usually responds to commands and when mentioned or replied to; the owner must enable a group with `/enablegroup`.

## OriginOS & customization

`/island` · `/islandmusic` · `/originplayer` · `/originos` · `/latest` · `/funtouch` · `/region` · `/aod` · `/notifications` · `/background`

## Devices & updates

`/lookup vivo V30` · `/lookup iQOO 12` · `/updates` · `/beta` · `/iqoo` · `/flashcharge` · `/battery`

## Tips & troubleshooting

`/games` · `/camera` · `/gcam` · `/storage` · `/ram` · `/debloat` · `/apk` · `/bugs` · `/ask` · `/support` · `/rules`

## AI & privacy

`/about` · `/usage` · `/privacy` · `/forget` · `/memory on|off` · `/language auto|en|ms|hi|id|zh|ta|ar`

## Utilities

`/banner` · `/polls`

## Admin / owner-only

`/admin` lists available commands, including `/settings`, `/set`, `/enablegroup`, `/disablegroup`, `/chatid`, `/warn`, `/unwarn`, `/ban`, `/unban`, `/info`, `/reset`, `/clear`, `/setbanner`, and `/poll`.
